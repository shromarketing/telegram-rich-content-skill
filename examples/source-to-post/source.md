## Raw thought

People see Telegram Rich Messages and immediately want every post to become a mini-site.
That is backwards. Start with the reader and the job. A short update should remain a
short normal post. A photo sequence often needs an album. Use a rich message only when
structure such as a table, expandable detail, or slideshow makes the idea easier to
understand. Whatever format we choose, validate it locally, test it in a private channel,
and ask again before production.
