Rich is not the goal. Clarity is.

Telegram can carry posts that feel like small editorial products. That does not mean every update needs every available block.

Choose the lightest format that improves understanding.
A practical format decision
FormatUse it when
Normal postOne compact idea needs broad compatibility.
AlbumA visual sequence matters more than long copy.
Rich MessageTables, expandable context, or a slideshow make the material clearer.
Why not make everything rich?

Formatting adds cognitive and compatibility cost. Each block should earn its place by helping the reader scan, compare, verify, or explore.

Keep the publishing gates separate

  • Validate locally.
  • Review in a private test channel.
  • Approve the exact production target immediately before sending.
The useful question is not “How rich can we make it?” It is “What makes this idea easier to understand and safer to publish?”
